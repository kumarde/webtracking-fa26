How the Web Tracks You / Targeted Advertising

UCSD CSE 190/291, Fall 2026

The goal of the course is to introduce students to the vast tracking ecosystem that underlies the web and give students experience implemented these technologies in practice. Specific topics will include technical introductions to tracking techniques including cookies and browser fingerprinting, the mechanics of ad auctions, as well as marketing databases and targeting inferences. Students will also learn about the history of advertising, tactics companies use to persuade consumers, and ethical implications of a surveillance capitalism economy.

Students will learn the fundamentals of the web (i.e., the protocols and infrastructure that support the web today), how privacy is (or isn’t) protected today, and get hands-on experience (through PAs) implementing cookie-based tracking and inference, browser / device fingerprinting, and ad blocking technologies.

To complete the projects in this course, you will need to have (or gain) familiarity with all aspects of web programming, not limited to — web server deployment, JavaScript, web databases (e.g. SQL), client/server applications, and web extensions. We will not explicitly teach these in class and you will be expected to pick up what you need for each project on your own.

Course Structure

This is a typical project and exam structured course. Grading is based on the following components:

Attendance (5%)

Attendance is mandatory in class and constitutes 5% of your grade. You can miss up to 2 classes without penalty and without needing to justify your absence. If you have additional conflicts, please contact staff on Piazza.

Programming Assignments (40%)

There are three programming assignments (PAs) in the course. The first is worth 18% of your grade, the second is worth 12% of your grade, and the third is worth 10% of your grade. The schedule of release and due dates for PAs are displayed below. All assignments MAY be done in teams of two, but no more than two per team. Teams can change from PA to PA.

Midterm (20%)

There will be one in-class midterm (90 minutes) in week 6 on 11/5 . The midterm will cover material from all the lectures, readings, and programming assignments.

Final (35%)

The final exam will be: Undergraduate students (CSE190): December 10th, 2026, from 3pm to 6pm. Graduate students (CSE291): December 11th, 2026, from 11:30am to 2:30pm.

The final exam will be comprehensive over the whole course, but with material weighted towards the second half of the course. We will automatically substitute the midterm grade with your final grade if you score on the final is higher than the midterm.
